Automation Project Life Cycle

Granthium Automation is an independent automation solutions provider, experienced across a wide range of leading controller platforms. Our services cover the complete automation project lifecycle — from conceptual design and detailed engineering to programming, panel assembly, and on-site implementation. We deliver PLC-based process and machine control systems with integrated visualization and data acquisition solutions.

Frontend & Systems Design

Define project scope, system architecture, and component selection. Develop RFQs, technical specs, functional designs, power/control schematics, BOMs, and test procedures to ensure reliable automation.

Programming & Configuration

Develop and implement PLC and HMI programming, develop simulation models, and conduct FAT/IFAT factory testing for comprehensive system validation.

Field Installation

Perform electrical wiring and instrument setup on-site. Integrate system components and verify connections and communication between devices.

Testing, Commissioning & Support

Conduct comprehensive loop and IO checks, followed by SAT and ISAT validation. Provide thorough operator training and deliver ongoing maintenance and technical support post-commissioning.

SWITCHBOARDS & CONTROL CABINETS

Custom built cabinets for every industry need

With over six decades of combined expertise in industrial automation, our team specializes in the design, assembly, and testing of high-performance PLC and conventional control cabinets. Built at our 1,500 sq. ft. Mussafah-based facility, each cabinet is tailored to meet local and international standards and undergoes thorough QA/QC validation before delivery. Our infrastructure is equipped for staging, simulation, and FAT/IFAT testing to ensure readiness for deployment.



Industries Served:

  • Oil & Gas: PLC, ICS, ESD, F&G, RTU, IRP, ExnR/ATEX certified cabinets, network & marshalling panels, intelligent MCCs, integrated power/control cabinets
  • Power Generation: Generator control/protection cabinets, AMF/synchronization systems, solar monitoring, battery chargers, substation panels
  • Water & Wastewater: Chlorination & pumping station cabinets, RO systems, HVAC controls, analyzer panels, LV MCCs
  • Infrastructure & Industry: District cooling controls, baggage handling, tunnel ventilation, relay-based and irrigation system cabinets


Control System Upgrade Services

Legacy systems and obsolete components present a major challenge across industries. We provide turnkey upgrade services—partnering with OEMs to replace or retrofit outdated DCS, ESD, and F&G systems. Our scope covers both component-level enhancements and full system migrations.

  • Gas Turbine, HRSG, and Water Plant control system upgrades
  • LV Switchboard and MCC retrofits or full replacements
  • MV switchgear protection relay modernization
  • Field instrument upgrades and outage-based replacement planning

IIoT & Digital Solutions

Harnessing machine data for smarter operations

We deliver Industrial IoT (IIoT) solutions that enable real-time data acquisition, analytics, and visualization for operational efficiency and informed decision-making. Our team integrates machine-level PLCs and HMIs with enterprise-level IT systems to digitize plant performance tracking, streamline operations, and reduce downtime.

Through no-code platforms, we develop tailored dashboards that visualize KPIs such as availability, productivity, energy usage, and asset conditions—accessible both onsite and remotely via mobile devices. Our solutions are scalable across industries and can be deployed on-premises or cloud-based.

  • Real-time performance dashboards (OEE, downtime, energy)
  • Asset condition monitoring with predictive maintenance
  • Digital maintenance & mobile integration
  • Energy management and loss tracking
  • RFID/QR-based digital asset tracking
  • Cloud, edge, and AI/ML-powered analytics

Our IIoT platforms transform factories into intelligent, data-driven environments—improving productivity, reducing costs, and enabling agile decision-making.
