Industrial Automation

Granthium Automation is an independent automation solutions provider, experienced across a wide range of leading controller platforms. Our services cover the complete automation project lifecycle — from conceptual design and detailed engineering to programming, panel assembly, and on-site implementation. We deliver PLC-based process and machine control systems with integrated visualization and data acquisition solutions.

Frontend & Systems Design

Define project scope, system architecture, and component selection. Develop RFQs, functional designs, Schematics and GA designs, Cause & Effecct Matrix, IO and interface lists, power/control schematics, BOMs, and test procedures to ensure reliable automation.

Programming & Configuration

Develop and implement PLC and HMI programming, develop simulation models, and conduct FAT/IFAT factory testing for comprehensive system validation.

Field Installation

Perform electrical wiring and instrument setup on-site. Integrate system components and verify connections and communication between devices.

Testing, Commissioning & Support

Conduct comprehensive loop and IO checks, followed by SAT and ISAT validation. Provide thorough operator training and deliver ongoing maintenance and technical support post-commissioning.

MICROGRID SOLUTIONS

Our sustainable energy solution includes controls for Microgrids comprises of Solar Array, Battery Energy Storage Systems and Diesel Generator sets.

  • Hybrid Power Controller (Solar + BESS + DG): Our advanced hybrid controller serves as the "brain" of the system, intelligently orchestrating solar, battery, and diesel assets to maximize efficiency and minimize fuel consumption. It ensures a stable power supply by dynamically balancing load requirements with the most cost-effective energy source available.
  • Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) Integration: We provide seamless BESS integration to capture excess renewable energy and provide instantaneous frequency response and voltage support. This system acts as a critical buffer, smoothing out solar intermittency and providing reliable backup power during peak demand or outages.
  • Solar Inverter Control & Monitoring: Our solution offers high-precision control and real-time monitoring of solar inverters to ensure maximum power point tracking and grid compliance. By supervising string performance and thermal health, we optimize the total energy yield of your solar array.
  • Mode & Grid-Tied Seamless Transition: Our controls facilitate a "bumpless" transfer between grid-connected and islanded modes, protecting sensitive equipment from voltage fluctuations during a transition. This ensures your facility remains operational during utility grid failures without any perceptible loss of power.
  • Renewable Energy Forecasting & Management: Utilizing predictive analytics, our management system forecasts solar irradiance and load patterns to preemptively adjust energy reserves. This proactive approach reduces reliance on diesel generators and ensures the microgrid is always prepared for upcoming weather shifts.
  • Distributed Energy Resource (DER) Optimization: We employ sophisticated algorithms to optimize the dispatch of all distributed resources based on real-time electricity pricing and equipment health. This results in a lower Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE) while extending the operational lifespan of your hardware assets.

TRAINING & SIMULATION MODULES

We provide specialized Industrial Automation Training Modules designed to upskill engineers and technicians from OEMs and large-scale enterprises.

Our comprehensive kits feature integrated PLC, HMI, PC based SCADA and VFD architectures mounted on professional workbenches, providing a high-fidelity environment for hands-on assembly and programming experience for trainees.

Beyond training, these units serve as critical simulation hubs, allowing engineering team to validate and 'stress-test' modified code in a safe environment before deploying to live, real-time production systems.
