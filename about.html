About Us

Focused on delivering value through precision and performance.

Who We Are

Granthium Automation is a team of forward-thinking engineers, project managers, and technicians with deep expertise in electrical systems and automation. We specialize in providing integrated solutions in industrial automation, switchboard manufacturing, IIoT, and support services across sectors.


Established in 2021, our core purpose is to provide advanced technological solutions and services to industries at a faster pace and at affordable costs. Our focus remains on Industry 4.0 solutions for small to medium size industries in various verticals, wherein customers can benefit from leveraging our IIoT solutions to reduce costs and increase productivity and availability of their assets.

Our team includes a project manager having more than 20 years of experience in automation systems engineering, assembly, site installation, testing and commissioning besides managing small to medium size integrated EPC projects. Our engineers, supervisors, technicians have several years of a proven track record delivering successful projects in Power, Water, Oil & Gas industries.

Our cabinet assembly and testing facility at Mussafah Industrial area, Abu Dhabi is fully equipped to assemble control cabinets & LV switchboards. Services includes assembly, internal testing, staging, simulation, FAT & IFAT for control cabinets. All cabinets undergo stringent quality control processes specified by industry standards before dispatch to customer.

With our business partners' staff and our team comprised of experienced personnel, we can deploy upto 40 highly experienced E&I technicians on short notice.
