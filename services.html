Control Systems


We design, assemble, and commission advanced control system solutions tailored to your industry. From PLC and RTU cabinets to MCCs and integrated control panels, our solutions meet ATEX and other international standards, ensuring safety and efficiency in critical operations.

Field Installation


Our field team provides end-to-end electrical and instrumentation installation across oil & gas, utilities, and infrastructure. Services include cabinet mounting, cable laying, glanding, loop checks, and commissioning—all backed by experienced engineers and technicians.

On-Site Maintenance Support


We offer responsive maintenance and troubleshooting services for automation systems, including software backups, hardware replacement, and remote VPN support. Our team ensures minimal downtime and sustained performance of your plant operations.
