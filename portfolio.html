Oil & Gas

Reliable process control for critical oil and gas operations



We deliver end-to-end automation and electrical solutions across diverse industrial sectors—engineered for safety, compliance, and performance in both hazardous and standard environments. Our expertise spans:

  • PLC / RTU-based process controls and HMI/SCADA monitoring solutions
  • Hazardous area solutions including Ex e, de, and nR certified controls
  • DCS, ESD, and Marshalling cabinets, mimic, and annunciation panels
  • Switchboard manufacturing including Intelligent MCCs and VFD cabinets
  • Integrated Power & Control Cabinets and small power distribution boards
  • Unit Control Panels (UCP) for chillers, compressors, and fans
  • Communication infrastructure, PAGA systems, and network cabinets
  • Complete control system upgrades, retrofits, and switchboard modifications

Power Generation

Comprehensive control and monitoring solutions for conventional and renewable power systems



We offer robust and reliable automation solutions tailored for power generation applications—from diesel generators to micro grids and substations. Our control panels and switchboards are engineered for seamless performance, safety, and compliance with industry standards.

  • Generator Control Panels: Protection and control cabinets for DG sets.
  • AMF / ATS & Power Management: Auto mains failure and load transfer cabinets.
  • Synchronization & Distribution: Intelligent synchronization and distribution switchboards.
  • Micro Grid Solutions: Integrated controls for Solar, BESS, and DG hybrid systems.
  • Solar Control & Monitoring: Real-time analytics and monitoring dashboards.
  • Substation Automation: RTU and SCADA-based control systems and protection panels.
  • Auxiliary Systems: Battery chargers, network cabinets, and energy monitoring.

Infrastructure & Utilities

Scalable automation solutions for critical infrastructure, water treatment, and data centers



We deliver specialized electrical and control systems designed for the rigorous demands of public utilities and critical infrastructure. Our solutions ensure 24/7 availability and seamless connectivity:

  • Water & Sewage Treatment: Control systems for pumping stations, chlorination, and treatment plants.
  • Data Center Infrastructure: Specialized fuel system cabinets and high-availability network cabinets.
  • Industrial Communication: PAGA systems, network cabinets, and site-wide connectivity solutions.
  • Power Distribution: Low Voltage (LV) switchboards, Intelligent MCCs, and lighting DBs.
  • Process Visualization: HMI/SCADA systems for real-time monitoring of utilities and district cooling.
  • Critical Upgrades: Full control system retrofits and switchboard modifications for aging infrastructure.
