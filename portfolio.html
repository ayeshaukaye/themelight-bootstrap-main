Oil & Gas

Reliable process control for critical oil and gas operations

We deliver end-to-end automation and electrical solutions for the oil, gas, and chemical sectors—engineered for safety, compliance, and performance in both hazardous and standard environments. Our expertise spans:

  • PLC / RTU-based process control and monitoring systems
  • Hazardous area (ATEX) certified control cabinets
  • Mimic, annunciation, and marshalling panels
  • Intelligent MCCs, VFDs, lighting & small power DBs
  • Unit Control Panels (UCP) for compressors, chillers, and fans
  • Vibration and condition monitoring solutions
  • Remote asset tracking and predictive maintenance dashboards
  • Complete E&IC installation, retrofits, and control system upgrades

Power Generation

Comprehensive control and monitoring solutions for conventional and renewable power systems

We offer robust and reliable automation solutions tailored for power generation applications — from diesel generators to solar power plants and substations. Our control panels and switchboards are engineered for seamless performance, safety, and compliance with industry standards.

Through no-code platforms, we develop tailored dashboards that visualize KPIs such as availability, productivity, energy usage, and asset conditions—accessible both onsite and remotely via mobile devices. Our solutions are scalable across industries and can be deployed on-premises or cloud-based.

  • Generator Control Panels: Protection & control cabinets for DG sets
  • AMF/ATS & Power Management: Auto mains failure and load transfer cabinets
  • Synchronization Panels: Intelligent synchronization and distribution switchboards
  • Solar Monitoring: Real-time solar power generation and analytics dashboards
  • Substation Automation: RTU and SCADA-based control systems for substations

Infrastructure

Scalable automation solutions for critical infrastructure and industrial operations

We deliver comprehensive control and monitoring systems designed to optimize performance, ensure safety, and enhance operational visibility across diverse infrastructure projects. From smart plant controls to real-time analytics, our solutions are engineered for reliability and efficiency in demanding environments.

  • Manufacturing Intelligence Platforms with OEE, RCA & energy dashboards
  • Asset condition monitoring and digital asset tracking systems
  • Conventional MCCs, VFD panels, lighting & small power distribution boards
  • Control systems for district cooling plants and irrigation pump stations
  • Baggage handling and tunnel ventilation control solutions
  • Complete E&IC installation, retrofits, and control system upgrades
